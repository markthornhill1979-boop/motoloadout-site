Moto Loadout

Plan it. Pack it. Find it.

Organise your motorcycles, customise your luggage, plan what to take and keep track of where everything is packed. Moto Loadout makes preparing for your next trip easier.

Built for motorcycle touring, camping and adventure riding, with custom luggage locations, trip loadouts, packing checklists, Find My Gear and weight distribution tracking.

Get it on Google Play iPhone version coming soon

Available now on Android. The iPhone version has been submitted to Apple for review.

Plan it.

Add your bikes, create and name your own luggage and storage locations, build an equipment library and assign gear to each trip.

Pack it.

Use a packing checklist to see what is ready and what still needs packing, whether you are heading away for a weekend or a longer trip.

Find it.

Use Find My Gear to see which pannier, top box, tank bag or other storage location holds an item, without searching through every bag.

Built around real motorcycle travel

Moto Loadout is designed to help riders stay organised before they leave, while they are packing and when they need to remember where a piece of kit is stored.

  • Create fully custom luggage and storage setups
  • Add custom weights to luggage and equipment
  • Review weight distribution and spot possible imbalances
  • Access your loadouts offline, with no account required
