Moto Loadout

Plan it. Pack it. Find it.

Organise your motorcycles, customise your luggage, plan what to take and keep track of where everything is packed. Moto Loadout makes preparing for your next trip easier.

Built for motorcycle touring, camping and adventure riding.

Get it on Google Play iPhone version coming soon

Available now on Android. The iPhone version has been submitted to Apple for review.

Plan it.

Add your bikes, create and name your own luggage and storage locations, build an equipment library and assign gear to each trip. Add item and luggage weights to review how your load is distributed.

Pack it.

Use a packing checklist to see what is ready and what still needs packing, whether you are heading away for a weekend or a longer journey.

Find it.

Use Find My Gear to see which pannier, top box, tank bag or other storage location holds an assigned item, without searching through every bag.

Your packing information is available offline, with no account required.
