Moto Loadout Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 September 2026

Moto Loadout respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Moto Loadout mobile application.

Information you enter into Moto Loadout

Moto Loadout allows you to enter information such as motorcycles, luggage, equipment, trips, packing lists, weights, notes and related trip-planning data.

This information is stored locally on your device. Moto Loadout does not currently require a user account and does not operate a cloud database for this information.

Personal data collection

Moto Loadout does not currently collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Moto Loadout does not use advertising identifiers and does not contain third-party advertising.

Analytics and tracking

Moto Loadout does not currently use third-party advertising analytics or cross-app tracking.

Device storage

App data is stored locally on your device using the application's local database. Deleting the Moto Loadout app may also delete locally stored Moto Loadout data, depending on your device and operating system.

Data retention and deletion

Because Moto Loadout currently stores your app data locally rather than in a Moto Loadout cloud account, you can remove stored information by deleting items within the app or by uninstalling the app from your device.

External services

Moto Loadout may allow you to open external links using your device's browser or other installed applications. Any external websites or services you choose to access are governed by their own privacy policies.

Distribution platforms such as Apple App Store and Google Play may independently collect information relating to downloads, purchases, diagnostics or use of their services. Their collection and use of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Children

Moto Loadout is intended for adult motorcycle riders and is not directed toward children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Moto Loadout's features or data practices change. The current version will always be published on this page with the applicable effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Moto Loadout's handling of information, contact:

support@motoloadout.app
